## Problem

Retention and export/erase jobs are not sufficient when datasets lack an
accountable purpose, provider egress is not classified, relational and semantic
lineage can diverge, or local audit evidence can be altered without detection.
These gaps make recovery and data-rights receipts incomplete even if each store
is individually reachable.

## Scope

- Add a versioned data catalog for every persistent dataset and derived flow,
  including owner, purpose, classification/PII posture, retention source,
  access audience, residency, legal-hold behavior, rights handling, and review
  triggers. Legal-basis and regulatory fields are owner/counsel assertions, not
  generated compliance claims.
- Add an approved subprocessor/destination registry and enforce provider,
  region, retention, and transfer constraints by server-derived data class
  before egress.
- Add SQLite/PostgreSQL-parity constraints or server-enforced composite
  invariants for trace/task/session/artifact/message/approval lineage, with
  deterministic quarantine and reconciliation for legacy orphans.
- Preserve immutable approval request, decision, and note records; persist
  message parent lineage and choose one authoritative policy-decision source
  while leaving the public `message.*` contract unchanged.
- Make local audit segments metadata-minimized and tamper-evident through
  ordered, candidate-versioned integrity links and operator-verifiable
  checkpoints rooted in durable operation/outbox state.
- Register metadata-only data-quality SLIs for orphan rate, coherence,
  duplicate operations, retention overdue, export/erase completion, backup
  age, Redis lag, audit parity, and store growth through `E/0/04`.
- Supply the catalog, lineage, audit-integrity, and quality evidence consumed
  by the final lifecycle/release gate `I/0/04`.

## Non-scope

- No real personal-data read or migration, universal compliance claim,
  unrestricted raw export, public `message.*` shape change, content-bearing
  telemetry, retroactive invention of missing lineage, or destructive repair
  without a reviewed quarantine receipt.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every durable dataset and egress destination has a validated accountable entry.
- [ ] Classification-derived provider/residency rules fail closed before egress.
- [ ] Referential and semantic lineage is coherent across SQLite and PostgreSQL.
- [ ] Approval, message-parent, and policy-decision history remains reconstructable.
- [ ] Audit deletion, insertion, reordering, or modification is detectable without raw export.
- [ ] Data-quality SLIs and release evidence are metadata-only and candidate-bound.
