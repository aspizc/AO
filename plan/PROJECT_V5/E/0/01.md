## Problem

An approval is unsafe if its action/repository/change digest can change between
request, operator inspection, and effect.

## Scope

- Persist server-derived immutable approval context: action, principal/target,
  repo/task/trace, canonical target branch/ref, algorithm-qualified full
  change/tree/evidence digest, requested effect, expiry, and status.
- Expose pending `list/show` only over the operator control transport.
- Render branch and full digest in the mandatory approval preview; abbreviated
  digests and caller-provided branch labels are never decision authority.
- Make exact duplicate request idempotent and changed reuse a conflict.

## Non-scope

- No grant in this sheet, no agent-supplied authority fields, and no generic
  payload/artifact reader.
