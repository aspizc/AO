runtime settings shared by direct, Redis, and MCP implementations.

- matching schema fixtures and tests
- `gateway/src/config.js`
- `gateway/package.json` and lockfile

## RED

ordering, or non-positive coordination bounds are accepted.

## GREEN

tombstone, and orphan retention settings have safe defaults and fail closed on
invalid values.
