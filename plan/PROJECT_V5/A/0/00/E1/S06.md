# V5 E1/S06 — Recipient-scoped, idempotent transport ACK

Status: **complete**

Depends on: E1/S05 and E0/S02.

## Goal

Acknowledge only deliveries belonging to the authenticated recipient inbox.

## Write scope

- ACK service method
- focused ACK/fence tests

## RED

Mixed batches partially mutate, cross-inbox or replacement credentials
succeed, exact retry fails inside its tombstone window, or unknown IDs pass.

## GREEN

Unique nonempty batches use the digest/scope fence and all-or-nothing queue ACK;
an exact tombstoned retry reports zero newly acknowledged.
