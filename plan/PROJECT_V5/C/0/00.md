## Problem

A green command is not credible if Node support is ambiguous, manifests depend
on local paths, or required suites may be skipped or collect zero tests.

## Scope

- Declare and enforce the supported Node/runtime matrix and portable dependency
  manifest.
- Add required-lane sentinels for zero tests, unexpected skips, unavailable
  services, and stale generated files.
- Make local and CI entry points execute the same authoritative suite set.

## Non-scope

- No feature behavior change, broad dependency upgrade, or contact with shared
  Redis/MCP services.

## TDD GREEN

Add version/manifest validators and explicit required-suite accounting to the
single CI entry point.

Trial 6 GREEN establishes serial Linux subreaper containment, pidfd-only
signalling and reaping to a two-scan fixed point, plus an explicit post-freeze
caller handoff which restores the exact entry signal mask and handlers. A
non-blocking process-wide lock and an empty-child precondition make that serial
ownership enforceable rather than advisory.

Trial 7 GREEN moves the subreaper domain into a fresh per-suite supervisor
which completes a `READY` handshake before the runner starts. The embedding
process never becomes a subreaper, and its unrelated children cannot be
discovered, signalled, or reaped by the suite. A stable helper pidfd carries
cancellation and reap authority. Provisional root pidfds survive opaque stat;
if root pidfd acquisition itself fails persistently, cleanup is limited to the
exact unreaped direct `Popen` child and later directly observed adopted
children. A private framed protocol publishes a result only with proof of root
reap, quiescence, pidfd closure, and subreaper restoration.

Trial 8 makes helper shutdown total with bounded EOF, TERM, KILL, and exact
reap, and defers all terminal-frame decoding until helper exit/reap and return
code validation. Trial 9 routes the sole helper-pidfd close through a one-shot
fail-closed primitive: close uncertainty remains `ProcessCleanupError`, never
retries a potentially reusable numeric descriptor, preserves causal context,
and emits no public JSON.
