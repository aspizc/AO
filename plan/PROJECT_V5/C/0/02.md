## Problem

Review, integration, promotion, and release claims can refer to different trees,
locks, suites, or tags, while dependency risk can be hidden behind an
unstructured or stale waiver.

## Scope

- Define one machine-readable candidate manifest with full commit/tree identity,
  base and branch, runtime and lock digests, the authoritative CI suite
  manifest plus non-refreshable topology/skip/readiness contract, and review
  evidence digests.
- Validate distinct `planned`, `implemented`, `reviewed`, `integrated`,
  `promoted`, and `released` states against repository refs and immutable
  evidence; no state is inferred from prose or another state.
- Scan the complete production dependency graph from reproducible locks and
  reject lock drift, missing locks, and every unaccepted high/critical finding.
- Require each accepted advisory to bind package/advisory, candidate and lock
  digest, owner, reachability rationale, compensating control, and expiry.

## Non-scope

- No automatic tag, push, publication, dependency-major upgrade, or readiness
  claim based only on a checklist/review filename.

## TDD RED

Exercise abbreviated or mixed SHAs, changed tree/lock/review evidence,
contradictory refs/status/checklists, tag outside the candidate, a new
production advisory, and waivers that are expired, ownerless, overbroad, or
bound to another lock.
