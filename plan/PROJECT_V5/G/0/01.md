## Problem

A disposable Redis connection per operation is simple but costly and lacks an
explicit reconnect, bounded concurrency, draining, and shutdown contract.

## Scope

- Introduce owned persistent command/blocking clients with lazy connect,
  reconnect state, bounded queues, backpressure, health, and graceful shutdown.
- Preserve operation-level isolation/fencing and explicit unavailable errors.
- Keep test/direct factory ownership and process signal handling deterministic.

## Non-scope

- No infinite request queue, hidden retry of non-idempotent operations, global
  singleton across test/Gateway instances, or shared-server restart.

## TDD RED

Cover concurrent connect, connection drop mid-command/block, queue saturation,
shutdown during receive, reconnect storm, double close, and listener/client
leaks.

## TDD GREEN

Implement a per-service client lifecycle/pool with bounded scheduler and clear
retry classification.

## Implementation evidence

- Each queue owns one lazy persistent command lane and one lazy persistent
  blocking lane; independent service/factory instances share no client.
- Command admission defaults to 64 active plus 256 queued operations. The
  serialized blocking lane admits one active plus 32 queued receives. All
  limits are positive startup configuration and saturation fails explicitly.
- Node-redis offline queuing and automatic reconnect are disabled. A dispatched
  operation is attempted once; a transport fault invalidates the lane and only
  a later operation performs one coalesced reconnect.
- `close()` is idempotent: it stops admission, rejects queued work, drains for
  the configured two-second default, then advances a lane-owned shutdown epoch
  that settles every remaining caller with `COORDINATION_UNAVAILABLE`.
  Eventual transport results/errors are consumed without crossing that fence.
- Late-connect cleanup distinguishes a pre-connect destroy attempt from the
  required post-handshake destroy by connection generation and lane phase, so
  a client cannot reopen after close.
- The lane-owned connect promise remains authoritative until `connect()`
  settles. Node-redis `isOpen=true`/`isReady=false` is treated as an in-flight
  handshake, never as permission to dispatch.
- The Trial 3 deterministic regressions preserve the earlier KO repros:
  ignored blocking cancellation, connect completion after close, and a
  successful `GET` result after the shutdown deadline, plus Redis-like and
  lock-matched node-redis stalled handshakes.
- The direct service, tool registry, stdin end, `SIGINT`, and `SIGTERM` carry
  the same ownership closure. `agents:events`, production `message.*`, and the
  shared MCP/Redis instance are unchanged.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Healthy operations reuse owned connections.
- [x] Backpressure rejects/bounds work before memory grows unbounded.
- [x] Reconnect never duplicates a non-idempotent semantic operation.
- [x] Shutdown drains/cancels boundedly and leaves no handles.
