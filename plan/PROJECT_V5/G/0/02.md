## Problem

Transport receive/ACK primitives do not define how a long-running consumer
handles transient failure, poison messages, crash recovery, or safe replay.

## Scope

- Add a supervised consumer runner with bounded retry/backoff, attempt metadata,
  poison threshold, body-safe DLQ record, and authorized replay.
- Verify participant/trace/scope context and idempotent business consume before
  transport ACK.
- Expose health/backlog/degraded state to operator inventory.

## Non-scope

- No automatic execution of message instructions, infinite poison loop, raw
  body in metrics/audit/DLQ metadata, or unauthenticated replay.

## TDD RED

Cover crash before/after consume/ACK, transient handler error, malformed/poison
envelope, duplicate delivery, stale lease, shutdown, DLQ replay mismatch, and
message-carried authority.

## TDD GREEN

Implement the abortable runner, retry classifier, durable consume receipt/DLQ
metadata, and operator-authorized replay command.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] At-least-once transport produces one idempotent business effect.
- [ ] Poison messages stop retrying and become diagnosable without body leaks.
- [ ] Replay is exact, authorized, auditable, and non-duplicating.
- [ ] Runner state appears in health/inventory.

## Verification

Run consumer clock/fault/idempotency/DLQ tests and Redis crash/reclaim
integration; then `bash scripts/ci.sh` and `git diff --check`.
