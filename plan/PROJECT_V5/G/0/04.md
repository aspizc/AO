## Problem

A known scope ID is not an admission credential. Closed collaboration needs
versioned membership, participant limits, and revocation without using lease
tokens as broad authority.

## Scope

- Define operator-issued, scope/version/audience/capability-bound,
  expiring/single-use admission invites.
- Enforce participant and registration/rate caps plus revocation at register.
- Keep discovery private to admitted members and expose safe operator inventory.

## Non-scope

- No public scope enumeration, invite in prompt/artifact/audit, invite as tool
  authority, cross-scope discover, or Internet identity-provider design.
