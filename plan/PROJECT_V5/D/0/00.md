## Problem

Caller-supplied agent, role, repository, task, and trace values can be confused
with server-established authority or target identity.

## Scope

- Establish a connection/request `RequestContext` with authenticated principal,
  audience, capabilities, trace, task, and canonical realpath repository.
- Derive effective bindings server-side and treat caller identity/repo fields as
  assertions that may deny, never grant.
- Resolve actions through the canonical closed catalog; a missing, unknown, or
  version-skewed action is an explicit `DENY`, never a default policy path.
- Enforce capability, lineage, revocation, and cross-context checks before side
  effects.

## Non-scope

- No public child/reviewer bearer handles, implicit trust from coordination
  presence, fail-open compatibility for new actions, or authorization encoded
  only in prompts.

### Trial 4 correction

The service and the Claude, Codex, and Gemini adapters now fail closed on every
non-null execution binding unless it is server-owned and matches the complete
effective tuple: control action, assigned target agent/role/action, repository,
trace, task, and canonical cwd. The service performs this check before adapter
lookup, session persistence, or model-resolution audit and uses only the
verified binding tuple afterward. Adapters repeat the check before their
launch boundary; only `null`/`undefined` retains the legacy policy path.

The regression matrix covers spawn and delegate across all three real dry-run
adapters, including action, agent, role, repository, trace, task, cwd, cloned
object, and non-object non-null mutations. It asserts no adapter lookup or
invocation, session row, or audit mutation of any kind on pre-authority
rejection. Canonical symlink aliases resolving to the same cwd remain valid, as
does the positive orchestrator-to-assigned-planner path; post-authority policy
or execution failures retain their normal error audit.
