# A_0_2 — Generic AO workflows: operator decision

Recorded on 2026-10-07 from the operator's direct clarification. This records
human intent and does not constitute an agent-issued approval.

AO must be generic and usable by people working on different project types.
KYA is the project where the operator used it most; useful configurations and
ways of working must be retained and generalized. Specifically named examples
are defining waves for parallel work, launching Gateways by wave instead of
individual task, and generating epics and their stories.

Disposition: extract useful behavior into generic AO workflows, configurable
for each project. Preserve that knowledge while removing personal IDs, paths,
project-specific commands and implicit KYA defaults. KYA may remain historical
provenance or an optional example; it does not define the public product.
The operator did not answer the separate question about historical-document
retention in this message. That part remains open, without blocking the
independent workflow extraction and planning work.
